## The Context

The site engineers had no clear view of where and when revenue was being lost across inverters, combiners, weather stations and turbines.

## Our Intervention

We led the development, design, and project management of an intense development cycle across two years with a small team. We developed internal diagnostic tooling that helped the site engineers figure out where issues in their assets were arising, with novel data visualizations and modern dashboard tooling.

## The Result

The tool is being actively built upon to this day. The users have already found and ticketed 35+ performance issues, and have discovered problems worth $100,000 USD.
