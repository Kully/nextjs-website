The Company We Keep

Our Story

We are a boutique design & visualization shop with expertise in turning data into visual artifacts to support reasoning through complex systems and relationships. Our main keyword to remember us by is "data visualization", we do far more than that.

Finding insights you didn't even know existed

Data is everywhere, and on it's own doesn't have meaning. It requires interpretation and context for it to become more meaningful. One of the most impactful approaches to discover meaning from data is to play with visualization. Companies and individuals who can transform their data — and even their thoughts and ideas — into meaninful visual artifacts can improve their own decision-making, bring clarity to underlying problems, and develop an edge against their competitors who can't do this as efficiently.

Visualization, with a human touch

In our new world with AI and LLM tools, it's possible to go faster than ever in fully realizing and creating an idea. However, it is still insight and taste from human minds that turns any output into something truly meaningful and beautiful. Finding the right people to work with while leveraging new techniques, methodologies, and technology, while remaining open to learning can lead to a truly strong combination.

A First principles and generalist approach

Pattern recognition is our specialty. Since data exists across disciplines, you can apply this kind of pattern thinking in a renewables project or an NGO startup.

Some of our design heroes include Christopher Alexander (our favourite book: Notes On The Synthesis Of Form) and Edward Tufte, the inventor of the Sparklines.
