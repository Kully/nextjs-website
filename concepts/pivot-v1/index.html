About

Hi, I'm Adam

I was the top contributor to the Plotly Python library during my years at Plotly between 2016 and 2019. I created key charts such as Sploms and Gantt charts to help the library hit more than 3 million downloads, and to date I am the #4 lifetime contributor to the Plotly Python library.

Since 2020, I have been running a small boutique design shop called Zyphr, helping small to medium sized businesses visualize their data. Our industries range from TechBio, Finance, Health and the Renewables space.
